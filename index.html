About Arc Clinic

Arc Clinic is a holistic wellness centre in Four Bungalows, Andheri West, Mumbai. We bring together physiotherapy, clinical Pilates, neuro rehabilitation, nutrition, occupational therapy, and lifestyle medicine under one roof.

Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that true health is not simply the absence of pain or illness. It is the freedom to move well, think clearly, eat nourishing food, and live fully. Every person who walks through our doors receives care that addresses the root cause, not just the symptom.

Whether you are recovering from an injury, navigating a chronic condition, or simply wanting to live with more energy and less discomfort, our team of specialists works together to design a path that is personal to you.
